Studio Subasio — Assisi, Umbria

Two studios, one hillside.

Nome Uno and Nome Due work a few streets apart below the basilica, in stone rooms that once stored oil and grain. This is a placeholder catalogue for the studio's website — a working index of what they make, kept in the pale tones of Monte Subasio limestone.

Browse the five bodies of work below, in the order a visitor would meet them on a studio walk-through.

  • 01

    Paintings

    Oil and tempera studies of the Umbrian hills, worked in close, muted layers.
  • 02

    Graphic Design

    Posters, identity systems and printed matter made for local institutions.
  • 03

    Silhouettes

    Large-format photographic paper works built around the human figure in outline.
  • 04

    Mosaics

    Tessera work drawing on the region's Roman and medieval floors.
  • 05

    Photography

    Black-and-white studies of stone, light and the Assisi countryside.
