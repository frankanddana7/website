About the studio

Studio Subasio is a shared workspace on the lower slopes of Monte Subasio, a short walk from Assisi. It belongs to two artists who trade ideas, tools and the same north-facing light.

Nome Uno

Nome Uno paints and builds mosaics, working mostly from the surrounding hillside — stone, plaster, and the changing colour of the valley through the seasons.

Nome Due

Nome Due works across graphic design and photography, including a long-running series of large-format silhouette prints made in the studio's courtyard.
