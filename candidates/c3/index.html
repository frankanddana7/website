Two artists, one studio, the hills of Assisi.

Studio Subasio is the shared workspace of Nome Uno and Nome Due, painters and makers working in the shadow of Monte Subasio in Umbria, Italy. Below you'll find a growing collection of paintings, graphic design, silhouette studies, mosaics, and photography.
