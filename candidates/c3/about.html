About the Studio

Studio Subasio sits just outside Assisi, in the stone farmhouses and quiet light of Umbria. It is the shared home of two artists whose work moves between paint, print, and photograph.

Nome Uno

Nome works primarily in oil and mixed media, drawn to the layered colour of the Umbrian hillsides. Their paintings and mosaics often begin as small studies made outdoors before growing into larger studio pieces.

Nome Due

Nome's practice spans graphic design, photography, and large silhouette studies on photographic paper. A background in editorial design shows up in the clean geometry of even the most painterly work.
