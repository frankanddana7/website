About

Nome Uno & Nome Due

We are two artists based near Assisi, in Umbria, working a short walk apart and sharing a single studio where the seasons of the mountain set the pace of the work.

Nome Uno

Nome Uno trained as a painter and has spent two decades working in oil and pigment, drawing on the colour of the Subasio stone and the long light of the Umbrian valley. Recent work moves between canvas and large-format paper.

Nome Due

Nome Due works across photography, mosaic and graphic design, often returning to the human figure — most recently through a series of large silhouette works on photographic paper.

Studio Subasio is a placeholder name for this collaborative studio, used here until the artists choose their own.
