Umbria, Italy

Two artists working in the hills above Assisi

Nome Uno and Nome Due share a studio at the foot of Monte Subasio, where the stone of the mountain finds its way into paint, paper and print. This index gathers the current bodies of work — choose a series below.

  1. 01 Paintings Oil and pigment studies of the Umbrian hills
  2. 02 Graphic Design Posters and print for local exhibitions
  3. 03 Silhouettes Large-format photographic paper works
  4. 04 Mosaics Stone and tile assemblages
  5. 05 Photography Field studies of light on stone
