Two artists, one studio, near Assisi

Studio Subasio is the shared workspace of Nome Uno and Nome Due, set among the hills of Umbria below Monte Subasio. Between them they work across paint, print, cut paper, tile, and film — this site is a quiet record of that work.

Paintings

Oil and tempera studies of the Umbrian landscape and its light.

Graphic Design

Posters, identities, and print work made for local clients and studio projects.

Silhouettes

Large-format photographic paper works built around the human silhouette.

Mosaics

Tile and stone fragment compositions, several set into local buildings.

Photography

Documentary and studio photography from around the region.
