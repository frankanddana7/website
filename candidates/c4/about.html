About the studio

Studio Subasio sits on a quiet lane below Monte Subasio, a short drive from Assisi. It has been the shared studio of Nome Uno and Nome Due since they converted an old stone outbuilding into a working space with north light.

Nome Uno

Nome Uno trained as a painter and has spent the last decade working between landscape studies and large-format silhouette pieces on photographic paper. Much of the work begins outdoors and is finished in the studio.

Nome Due

Nome Due works across graphic design, mosaic, and photography, moving easily between a client brief and a personal project. A background in printmaking shows up in the flat colour and clean edges of the design work.

The studio

Together they take on the occasional commission — a mosaic set into a courtyard wall, a poster for a local festival, a set of portraits — alongside the ongoing personal work shown on this site. Placeholder copy throughout; replace freely.
