About

Two artists, one studio

Studio Subasio sits on the lower slopes of Monte Subasio, a short walk from Assisi. Nome Uno and Nome Due have shared the space for over a decade, working independently but showing together.

Nome Uno

Nome Uno trained as a painter and has spent the last several years working between canvas and print. The work returns often to the colour of local stone and the geometry of the hill towns.

Recent projects include the Paintings and Graphic Design series shown on this site.

Nome Due

Nome Due works primarily in photography and mosaic, drawn to the way light falls across a wall at different hours. Large-format silhouette works are a recent direction, made on photographic paper in the studio's darkroom.

Recent projects include the Silhouettes, Mosaics and Photography series shown on this site.
