Umbria, Italy

Studio Subasio

A small studio near Assisi, at the foot of Monte Subasio, shared by two artists working across paint, print, paper and light. This is a placeholder site — every word and image here is a stand-in for the real thing.

Nome Uno and Nome Due make work that looks closely at the stone, light and silhouette of the Umbrian hillside, then carries it into painting, graphic design, mosaic and photography.

Projects

Paintings

Oils and gouache built from the colours of the hillside.

Graphic Design

Print and identity work for local growers and events.

Silhouettes

Large photographic paper works with human silhouettes.

Mosaics

Tesserae in local stone, set by hand.

Photography

Quiet studies of light on stone and olive groves.
