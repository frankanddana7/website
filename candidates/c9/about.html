About the Studio

Two artists, one studio, Umbria

Studio Subasio sits on the hillside below Monte Subasio, a short walk from Assisi. Nome Uno and Nome Due have shared the space for over a decade, working separately most days and occasionally trading tools across the room.

01

Nome Uno

Nome Uno trained as a painter and has kept a daily practice ever since, moving between oil, ink, and — more recently — cut-paper silhouette work. The Umbrian light and the stone of the hill towns are a constant reference.

02

Nome Due

Nome Due works across graphic design, mosaic, and photography, often documenting the studio's other projects as they go. A background in print design shows in the careful, restrained way the work is laid out and framed.
