About

Nome Uno & Nome Due

Studio Subasio is a shared studio just outside Assisi, Umbria, named for the mountain that overlooks it. Nome Uno and Nome Due have worked side by side there since finishing art school, trading techniques and the occasional argument about colour.

Nome Uno

Nome Uno paints and builds mosaics, drawn to the way stone holds light differently through the day. Their work has shown in a handful of small regional galleries and a great many kitchens.

Nome Due

Nome Due works across photography, graphic design and the silhouette series, usually with a camera in one hand and a coffee in the other. Umbria's hill towns are a constant subject.
