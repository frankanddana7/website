Assisi · Umbria

Studio Subasio

Two artists, one studio at the foot of Monte Subasio. Nome Uno and Nome Due make paintings, silhouettes, mosaics, graphic design and photography, all shaped by the stone and light of Umbria.

The work

Five ongoing bodies of work. Filter by category, or scroll through everything below.

Paintings

Layered oil and acrylic studies built from the palette of the Umbrian hills.

Graphic Design

Posters, identities and print work for local makers and small studios.

Silhouettes

Large-format photographic paper works exploring the human silhouette.

Mosaics

Stone and tile mosaics assembled by hand, piece by piece.

Photography

Landscape and architectural studies of Assisi and the Umbrian valley.
