About the studio

Studio Subasio is the shared workshop of two artists, Nome Uno and Nome Due, working in the hills below Assisi, Umbria.

Nome Uno trained as a painter and has spent the last decade working across canvas, print and mosaic, drawing on the colours of the local stone and the Umbrian landscape.

Nome Due works primarily in photography and design, documenting the region's light and architecture, and translating it into graphic and photographic pieces made in the studio.

Together they show new work through this site as it is made — from paintings and mosaics to photography, silhouettes and graphic design.
