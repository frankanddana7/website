Studio Subasio

We are two artists working near Assisi, in the hills of Umbria, Italy. Our studio sits below Monte Subasio, where the local stone colours our work in paintings, design, silhouettes, mosaics and photography. Browse the collections below.

Paintings

Oil and acrylic works inspired by the Umbrian countryside.

Graphic Design

Posters, identities and print pieces made in the studio.

Silhouettes

Large-format photographic paper works with human silhouettes.

Mosaics

Stone and tile mosaics built piece by piece, by hand.

Photography

Photographs of Assisi, its stone, light and landscape.
