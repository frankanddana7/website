Candidates

Nine skeletons for the Studio Subasio site. Each one is a complete, self-contained nine-page site using identical filenames, so any candidate can be promoted to the real site by moving its folder up. Every candidate is white-dominant with Monte Subasio stone accents, and is built mobile-first.

Modern · rounded corners · clearly AI-generated

Big radii, soft shadows, pill buttons, chunky sans, cards everywhere. Leans into the look on purpose.

C1 Simple The smallest version. No JavaScript at all, ~120 lines of CSS. Easiest of the nine to edit. C2 Complex Sticky condensing header, animated gradient hero, lightbox, scroll reveals, filter chips. C3 My call Quiet site, one memorable touch: a floating white card over a slowly drifting stone gradient.

Clean · timeless · floating boxes

White boxes on white, hairline stone borders, serif headings, barely any radius. Could have been designed in 2005 or 2035.

C4 Simple The smallest version. No JavaScript, ~120 lines of CSS. Restraint as the whole idea. C5 Complex Sticky desktop sidebar nav, masonry gallery, lightbox, scroll reveals, prev/next links. C6 My call Home page that rewards exploration — hover reveals a project preview; stacked inline on phones.

Editorial · exhibition catalogue (my third style)

No boxes, no cards, no shadows. Typography and whitespace on white, like a printed gallery catalogue. Numbered index, hairline rules, unframed images.

C7 Simple The smallest version. No JavaScript, ~120 lines of CSS. Home page is a numbered contents list. C8 Complex Full-bleed image moments, sticky section label, scroll progress rule, lightbox, reveals. C9 My call A printed catalogue that happens to scroll — numbered index, quiet progress rule, full-bleed images.

Every candidate has the same nine pages: home, about, and seven project templates — paintings, graphic design, silhouettes, mosaics, photography, and two spares ready to be renamed.

Placeholders to replace: the studio name “Studio Subasio”, the artist names “Nome Uno” and “Nome Due”, the contact details ciao@studiosubasio.example and +39 000 000 000, and every image in each candidate’s images/ folder.

To adopt a candidate, move the contents of its folder to the repository root, replacing this page.
